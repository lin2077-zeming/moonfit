Statement of provenance
=======================

The activity files in this directory were recorded by the project author using
an iGPSPORT cycle computer and a Huawei activity band, and exported by the
vendors' own companion applications. They are not third-party works and carry
no third-party licence.

The author dedicates all rights in these files to the public domain under CC0
1.0, so that anyone may reproduce the verification gates in this repository
without seeking permission.

Redaction
=========

Before being committed, every file was passed through
`scripts/redact_corpus.py`. That tool zeroes the device serial number and
shifts every coordinate by a fixed offset. `README.md` in this directory
records the exact offset and what was deliberately left alone.

The unredacted originals are excluded from version control by `.gitignore` and
are not distributed in any form.
